The Glass Sea

DraftNot established canoncoast · tidal magic

At dawn, the sea remembers how to be solid.

For a few quiet hours, the water becomes a single sheet of dark glass. Beneath it, the lights of the drowned city still burn. The people of Aster cross on foot, listening for the first sound of the tide returning.

Those who cross

01 is the only cartographer who has walked beyond the old bell tower. She leaves no marks on her maps where the water is deepest.

The 02 say the crossing ends when the bells begin. No one agrees on who rings them.

Unresolved thought
What if the glass preserves the memory of everyone who has crossed it?
