# Focus your workspace

Use **⌥⌘F**, **View → Toggle Workspace Focus**, the workspace focus control, or search for **Focus workspace** in ⌘K. At narrow widths the entry is in Workspace tools. Choose **Exit focus** or use the shortcut again to return.

Focus hides the navigation column, contextual inspectors, companion conversation and compact composer. Documents also hide their tab strip, entity subpage tabs and artwork control, leaving the current writing or specialized page available. Formatting, save failures and recovery remain visible. Deliberately opened split documents stay together as your working context.

This is temporary presentation. Sidebar width/collapse, inspector preferences, conversation state, selected records and Manual/AI mode are retained. Pending writing must save before toggling; unsent composer text is retained. A model response already running keeps a visible Stop control. Focus itself makes no model request.

An explicit inspector or navigation command can reveal those controls again. Settings and Worlds leave Focus when opened directly. A new launch starts outside Focus. Native window controls and the AI/Manual switch remain accessible.

Focus transitions respect Reduce Motion. Background captures verify settled layout; they do not establish animation feel, physical keyboard behavior or VoiceOver acceptance.
