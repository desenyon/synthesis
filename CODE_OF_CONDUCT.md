# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in Synthesis to be welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people using it.
- Respect that people's worlds and creative work are personal. Do not share someone's lore or screenshots of it without permission.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they deem harmful.

Report problems privately to the maintainer through [GitHub](https://github.com/desenyon). All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.
